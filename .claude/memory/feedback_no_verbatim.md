When you reuse something the user wrote, in any kind of output, rephrase
it in your own sentences.

**Why:** The user is not a native English speaker and does not always know
the correct or best wording, so turning their suggestions into
well-written text helps.

**How to apply:** Individual words can be reused, as long as the
phrasing is your own.
