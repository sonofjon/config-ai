Never use the user's suggestive text verbatim in code, comments, or any output. Always rephrase in your own words. This applies to rough suggestions and hints at direction, not to precise terms or phrases the user deliberately chose; keep those, since rephrasing them tends to lose precision.

**Why:** The user is not a native English speaker and does not always know the correct or best wording, so turning their suggestions into well-written text helps.

**How to apply:** When the user suggests wording (e.g., for a comment or message), use it as guidance but write it in your own words. Keep technical terms and phrases that are clearly the most precise way to say it, and fit them into your own sentence.
